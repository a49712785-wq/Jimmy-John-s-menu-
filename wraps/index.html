Jimmy John's Wraps Menu: Prices & Calories (2026)

Quick Answer

Same price, different nutrition — that's the real choice here. All three regular wraps run $13.30, so pick by what you actually need: the Tuscan Italian Wrap for protein (44g, the best value at 3.31g per dollar), or the Kickin' Ranch Chicken Wrap if you're watching sodium (1,990mg, the lowest of the three). The limited-time Buffalo Ranch Chicken Wrap is the lightest on calories (370–710) but its price and full nutrition aren't published yet.

Wraps rotate more often than Jimmy John's sub lineup, so treat the Buffalo Ranch Chicken Wrap's availability as store-dependent — the other three are permanent, and their pricing and nutrition below are exact figures, not estimates.

Unwich: The Lettuce Wrap Option

Every wrap on this list can be ordered as an Unwich, which swaps the flour tortilla for crisp iceberg lettuce — the same low-carb format Jimmy John's uses for its subs. It cuts calories by whatever the tortilla itself was contributing and drops the added carbs to under 1g, since lettuce carries almost none. It's a common request from customers following low-carb, keto, or gluten-conscious eating patterns, though the kitchen cannot guarantee a gluten-free environment.

Wrap Combo: Pricing and What's Included

A Wrap Combo pairs any wrap with a side and a 22 oz fountain drink. Side options usually include a bag of Jimmy Chips, a pickle spear, or a cookie.

Allergen Information

Jimmy John's wraps commonly contain wheat, gluten, milk, egg, and soy through the tortillas, cheese, and dressings. Cross-contact with peanuts and tree nuts is possible in stores that prepare other menu items nearby. Anyone with a food allergy should ask staff for current allergen sheets, since recipes and suppliers can change.

Ordering Tips: Pickup vs. Delivery

Wraps travel well because the flour tortilla holds up better over time than French bread, which makes them a solid pick for delivery or catering orders. For the freshest texture, pickup and eat within 15–20 minutes of preparation. Mobile ordering through the Jimmy John's app lets you save customizations and reorder your usual wrap in a few taps.
