Jimmy John's sits in the middle of the sandwich-chain price range. Its standard sandwich runs 8 inches, longer than Subway's 6-inch default but shorter than a 12-inch footlong.

Low-Carb, Gluten-Free & Vegetarian

Jimmy John's Unwich® & Dietary Quick Guide

An Unwich swaps the bread for lettuce on any sandwich, dropping the added carbs to under 1g and cutting calories by roughly whatever the bread itself would have added. Jimmy John's has no dedicated gluten-free bread, so the Unwich is the standard low-carb and gluten-free substitute — though shared prep surfaces and slicers mean it reduces, not eliminates, cross-contact risk for celiac disease or a severe gluten sensitivity. For vegetarians, the #6 Veggie is the standing meat-free Original, and most other sandwiches can be built vegetarian on request by removing the meat.

Feeding A Group

Jimmy John's Catering & Party Box Pricing

An 18-piece party box typically feeds up to 9 people; a 30-piece box (10 sandwiches, cut into thirds) feeds 10–15 and generally runs $65–$110 depending on sandwiches and location. Boxed lunches (sandwich, chips, pickle, cookie per person) run $8–$14 per person. Order through the app or website, or call your store directly for same-day requests. See the complete catering guide for the full breakdown.

Ordering Smart

How to Get the Best Jimmy John's Prices

Ordering direct through the app or website is usually cheaper than DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub, which add their own service and delivery fees — third-party apps are worth it mainly for their own promo codes or when you're outside Jimmy John's own delivery radius. Many locations also offer a combo upgrade — chips and a drink added to any regular sandwich for close to a dollar more, though the exact price varies by store, so confirm it at checkout. The $8.99 Meal Deal (Pepe, Turkey Tom, or Vito with chips and a drink) is the most consistent nationwide value and needs no promo code. Freaky Fast Rewards, the free loyalty program, typically offers a free regular Original after your first $5+ purchase, plus a birthday reward once your birthday's on file.

How We Source & Verify This Menu Data

Every calorie count and nutrition figure on this page and in the decision tool above is cross-checked against Jimmy John's own official nutrition calculator, which is linked directly from Jimmy John's site footer and is the source Jimmy John's itself uses to publish per-item nutrition facts.

Prices are estimates, built from real-world ordering data rather than a single official price list, and they vary by location, franchise, and time — sometimes by a dollar or more on the same item. Treat every price on this page and in the tool as a starting point, not a quote, and confirm the exact price for your store in the app or at checkout before you order.

We verify in passes rather than trusting a single source: after compiling the full dataset, we spot-check a sample of items across every category directly against the official calculator, item by item. When a spot-check catches a mismatch, we fix it and note it — for example, an earlier audit pass found the Chicken Bacon Ranch's published calories and sodium were off, which we corrected sitewide. Where the official calculator doesn't list an item at all (a few limited-time or off-menu builds), we say so rather than estimate a number. This isn't a one-time export; we recheck periodically since recipes, prices, and the menu itself change over time.
