Jimmy John's Secret Menu: Every Hidden Sandwich Hack and How to Order It (July 2026)

Quick Answer

Jimmy John's has no official secret menu, but 10+ customer-built hacks work at almost any location because every ingredient is already on the public menu. Below is the exact base sandwich, the modifiers, and the wording to use so staff build it correctly the first time. Prices vary by store since these are all custom builds on top of regular menu items, not separate products with their own price tag.

One By One

Jimmy John's Secret Menu Items, Explained One by One

Every hack below started the same way: a regular customer combined existing Jimmy John's ingredients into something that wasn't on the board, and it caught on enough to get a nickname. None of these require a code word. Staff can build any of them correctly as long as you describe the base sandwich and the changes, so treat the nickname as a shortcut for your own memory rather than something you need to say out loud.

BLAHS

Named for its own ingredients, bacon, lettuce, avocado spread, hot peppers, and salami, the BLAHS takes a familiar sub and pushes it toward something spicier and creamier.

Base
J.J.B.L.T.
Changes
Remove tomato, add avocado spread, hot peppers, salami
Flavor profile
Salty, creamy, slow-building heat
How to order
"J.J.B.L.T., no tomato, add avocado spread, hot peppers, and salami."
Works on
French bread, wheat, wrap, or Unwich
Note
Avocado spread and hot peppers aren't stocked at every location. Confirm before ordering.

Noah's Ark

This build takes the already large J.J. Gargantuan and doubles every meat on it, which is why regulars treat it as two meals in one wrapper rather than a single lunch order.

Base
J.J. Gargantuan (salami, capicola, ham, roast beef, turkey, provolone)
Changes
Double every meat
Why people order it
Enough food for two meals, often split in half
How to order
"J.J. Gargantuan, double all the meat."
Works on
Regular or Giant, French or wheat, or Unwich
Note
Doubling five meats adds up in price. Ask for a total before committing.

Dubbuhgutbustuh (Double Gut Buster)

This is the largest build on the list by a wide margin, and it's meant for sharing or for genuinely big appetites rather than a solo lunch.

Base

Changes
Double meat + 2 orders of bacon (about 10 strips)

How to order

Works on

Highest add-on cost on this list. Confirm pricing with your store first.

DIY Picklewich

Changes
Add a whole dill pickle on the side
Background

How to order

Works on

LBI (Leave Bread In)

By default, workers scoop some bread out of the roll to make room for fillings. Skipping that step is a free request that noticeably changes the texture of any sub.

Base
Any sub on French or wheat bread
Changes
Nothing removed, just skip the default bread-scooping step
How to order
"Leave the bread in" or "don't gut the bread."
Cost
Free
Note
Doesn't apply to Little Johns, since those rolls aren't scooped by default.

How to Order Off the Jimmy John's Secret Menu

The method you use to order changes how reliably a hack comes out right. Nicknames don't always land with staff, especially at busy locations or with newer hires, so the safest approach is describing the sandwich the same way it's written above.

How to Order Secret Menu Hacks
MethodWhat To Do
In-storeRead off the base sandwich, then each modification. Skip the nickname.
App or onlineUse "Customize" on the base sandwich and check off each ingredient change manually. Most reliable for complex builds.
PhoneSame approach as in-store: base sandwich first, then changes one at a time.

Jimmy John's Secret Menu Prices and Calories

None of these hacks carry a fixed, published price or calorie count, since none of them are official menu items with their own listing. What you're paying is the base sandwich price plus whatever gets added, and that add-on pricing depends on your store. See the base prices on the Jimmy John's Sandwich Menu and full macros on the Jimmy John's Nutrition Guide.

Cost formulaBase sandwich price + add-on charges, which vary by store and state
Bigger base = higher add-on costDoubling meat on a Gargantuan costs more than doubling meat on a Little John, since the starting portion is bigger
Lightest buildSlim BLT, a stripped-down Plain Slim with no cheese and no produce
Heaviest buildDubbuhgutbustuh, double meat, 10 strips of bacon, Giant bread, roughly 1.75 lbs

For an exact total, ask your local store to ring up the modified order. That reflects real add-on pricing and official ingredient nutrition rather than an estimate. Final Thoughts

Jimmy John's secret menu is a set of smart combinations built entirely from ingredients the chain already sells, not a hidden system with its own rules.
