Jimmy John's Catering Menu: Prices, Options, and How to Order

Quick Answer

$8 to $14 per person for box lunches, $85 to $110 for a medium party platter, and $40 to $200 for a full catering bundle: that's the general range you're working with when you order Jimmy John's for a group. Where you land in that range depends on group size, sandwich picks, and location.

Here's what's actually on the catering menu, what each format costs, and how to order it so it shows up on time and still tastes fresh.

What's on the Jimmy John's Catering Menu

Jimmy John's catering splits into two main formats, depending on group size and how formal the event is.

Box Lunches

Individually boxed meals are the easiest option for meetings, since everyone gets their own sandwich, chips, a pickle spear, and a cookie without any sharing or cutting required. You typically choose from a set list of classic sandwiches (like the Turkey Tom, Italian Night Club, or Vito) pulled from the same full sandwich lineup offered in-store, and each guest can usually pick their own bread and sandwich type when the order is placed in advance.

Box lunches work best for:

Corporate meetings and interviews Small team lunches (5–20 people) Groups with different dietary preferences

Party Platters and Box Party Subs

For bigger gatherings, Jimmy John's offers party sub platters, which are made from their full-length sandwiches sliced into smaller portions and arranged on trays. These are meant to be shared buffet-style, so they pair well with sides ordered separately.

Party platters are the better fit for:

Office parties and birthdays Sports-watching gatherings Self-serve events

Bags of chips (regular or jalapeño kettle chips) Pickle spears Cookies, brought in trays for group orders Bottled drinks or canned soda

How Much Does Jimmy John's Catering Cost?

Pricing depends on your location, the sandwiches selected, and current promotions, so the most accurate quote comes from your nearest store or the online ordering page. As a general guide, box lunches are priced per person, while party platters and bundles are priced per tray or per bundle and typically feed a set number of guests.

How to Order Jimmy John's Catering

  1. Pick your format. Decide between box lunches (individual, best for meetings) and party platters (shared, best for casual gatherings).
  2. Confirm your headcount. Most locations ask for a minimum order size for catering, so have a rough guest count ready before you call or order online.
  3. Place the order early. Catering isn't a walk-in order. Same-day requests are sometimes possible for smaller groups, but 24 to 48 hours of notice gives the store time to prep everything properly, especially for larger platters.
  4. Choose pickup or delivery. Availability depends on the store, so check your nearest location and hours and confirm delivery windows and any delivery fees when you place the order.
  5. Double-check dietary needs. If your group has vegetarian eaters or anyone avoiding specific meats, ask about substitutions when ordering, since not every location lists these options online.
Before You Order

Tips for a Smoother Catering Order

Mix Bread Types

If you're ordering box lunches for a group, offering a mix of wheat, white, and wrap options keeps most preferences covered without extra cost.

Order Slightly More Chips Than Sandwiches

Chips run out faster than people expect at group events.

Check Nutrition Info Ahead of Time

If anyone in the group has allergies or is tracking intake, the nutrition page breaks down ingredients and calories by sandwich, useful for building a box lunch list that covers different needs.

Call Ahead for Large Orders

Anything above 20 to 25 people is usually handled more smoothly over the phone with the store manager than through the standard online flow.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Delivery availability depends on the specific store location and how far the venue is from it. It's best to confirm delivery range when placing the order rather than assuming it's included.

Most locations set a minimum headcount or dollar amount for catering orders, particularly for party platters. Check with your local store, since this can vary by market.

Standard catering menus stick to a set list of sandwiches, but many locations will accommodate simple swaps (like removing an ingredient) if you ask when ordering.

For anything over 15 to 20 people, ordering at least a day ahead is the safer bet. Smaller box lunch orders can sometimes be placed same-day, depending on store capacity.

Jimmy John's catering menu is built for speed, which fits the brand's whole reputation for fast sandwiches. Box lunches make sense when everyone needs their own meal, and party platters work better for casual, shared setups. Either way, ordering a day ahead and confirming your headcount with the store avoids most of the last-minute scrambling that comes with feeding a group.
